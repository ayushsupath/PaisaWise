## Product concept

PaisaWise is a fictional, free financial-literacy website created for the Financial Markets & Institutions assignment. It gives Indian college students and first-jobbers a friendly place to learn everyday money skills alongside concepts from India’s financial system. Lessons and interface content are available in English and Hindi.

The tagline is **“Smart money, smarter you.”** It connects practical money knowledge with personal confidence: learning about money is not about getting rich quickly, but about making more informed choices at college and at the start of a career.

## Audience and benefits

The advertisement is made for students and first-jobbers aged 18–24, including people managing hostel or paying-guest rent, a first stipend, UPI spending, or an early salary. PaisaWise offers short lessons in both languages, a quiz to check understanding, a browser-based budgeting tool, and an interactive Market Lab for exploring money-market instruments. It can be used without creating an account.

The ad appears in A4 poster, square social post, vertical story, and wide-banner formats. Each version includes the product name, tagline, audience, benefits, a call to action, and the disclaimer below.
